# 3-minute walkthrough — script and shot list

Record only after a real sync, and replace the bracketed items with what your data actually shows. Do not narrate figures you have not clicked through.

**0:00–0:20 — Intro.** "Customer Success managers juggle accounts where some numbers are verified and some are just claims. This CRM keeps that difference visible." *Screen: Overview.*

**0:20–1:00 — Account one + five questions.** Go through the five panels on Overview for [Account 1]: pipeline split (Verified vs Unverified), power map, top three actions, risks, unknowns. Say one sentence per panel.

**1:00–1:35 — Trace a figure.** Click a [financial figure] → evidence drawer: status badge, status reason, source ID, excerpt. Say which document it comes from and whether it is VERIFIED or only a claim.

**1:35–2:15 — Discovery/discrepancy.** Open Risks & Deadlines → [discrepancy or unverified-figure risk]. Show the two evidence records side by side, with statuses. Explain the rule ID. *(Only use this segment if the rule actually fired on your data.)*

**2:15–2:45 — Account two + actions.** Switch to [Account 2] on Actions: top three, reason, owner, "Date not established" where applicable.

**2:45–3:00 — Architecture.** Synchronization page: next permitted call time, cached-vs-live indicator. "One serial queue, 65-second spacing, everything cached, token server-side."
